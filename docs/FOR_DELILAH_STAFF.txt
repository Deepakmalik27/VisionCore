HOW TO EXPORT CAMERA FOOTAGE — Delilah LA
==========================================
Takes about 10 minutes. No software to install.

WHAT WE NEED
------------
Date:    Monday, July 27, 2026
Time:    5:00 PM to 7:00 PM
Camera:  the one covering the FRONT DOOR / host stand
         (likely CAM.101, CAM.104, or CAM.115 — whichever shows the entrance)


STEPS
-----
1. Open  https://unifi.ui.com  and sign in
   (or open the UniFi Protect app)

2. Choose the console:  H.WOOD DLH LA - NVR.100.001

3. Click  Protect

4. In the left icon strip, click the  ▶  (play) icon — this is Live / Playback

5. Pick the front-door camera from the list

6. Scrub the timeline back to  July 27,  5:00 PM

7. Select a 10-MINUTE range first  (5:00 PM to 5:10 PM)

8. Click  Download / Export
   -> it saves an .mp4 to your computer

9. IF THAT WORKED, repeat for the rest:
      5:10-5:20,  5:20-5:30,  5:30-5:40 ... up to 7:00 PM
   (12 clips total)

   Please keep 10-minute pieces. Longer exports often fail partway
   and we end up with a corrupt file.

10. Upload the .mp4 files to Google Drive and share the link


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
"No footage at that time"
    -> the camera may not have been recording. Try a different camera,
       or tell us and we'll pick another date.

Export starts but never finishes
    -> the range is too long. Try 5 minutes instead of 10.

Cannot find the Download / Export button
    -> your account may be view-only. Ask whoever manages the
       UniFi system for export rights.


ONE MORE THING (10 seconds, very helpful)
-----------------------------------------
In Protect, go to:   Settings -> On-Site Retention

Tell us the number of days shown. We need to know how far back the
recordings go, so we know whether the July 27 footage is about to be
overwritten.
