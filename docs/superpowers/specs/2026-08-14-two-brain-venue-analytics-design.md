# Two-Brain Venue Analytics — Design

**Status:** proposed · **Date:** 2026-08-14 · **Camera:** CAM.112 (Delilah LA), generalising to any fixed camera

---

## 1. The goal, in plain English

1. Point a camera at a venue. Get back what a good manager would have seen if
   they had watched all day — without anyone watching.
2. The numbers must be trustworthy: how many guests, when they arrived, how long
   they waited, was the desk covered, who was staff.
3. It must beat a human eye — not by seeing better, but by never blinking and
   remembering every face for twelve hours.
4. Fast enough to be useful: a night reviewed before the next shift.
5. Works on any camera. Draw zones, go.
6. Says when it does not know. A confident wrong number is worse than "unmeasured".

## 2. The core principle: CASCADE

Spend compute in proportion to information. Cheap test first; defer to the
expensive model only when the cheap one is unsure.

    NoScope (VLDB)  up to 1000x on fixed-angle video
    BlazeIt         +40x on top of NoScope, "with no accuracy loss"

The enabling fact is ours: a FIXED camera watching a mostly-empty room. Paying
full price per frame for an empty lobby is the entire waste.

    TIER 0  decode + motion gate        ~free       skips empty hours
    TIER 1  detector (TensorRT)         cheap       is anyone there?
    TIER 2  IoU / motion association    ~free       unambiguous? STOP HERE
    TIER 3  ReID embedding              expensive   ONLY on ambiguity
    TIER 4  face / staff gallery        costlier    ONLY staff-zone candidates
    TIER 5  VLM narration               costliest   ONLY on flagged events

Tiers 0, 1, 4 already exist and work. Tier 2->3 gating and tier 5 do not.

## 3. Two brains, not one

The pipeline currently asks CLIP embeddings to do two unrelated jobs. On 95%
infrared footage it does neither well — this run's own words: "distributions
overlap, clip is not cleanly separating same/different person".

    CV PIPELINE (exists)   ->  WHO, WHERE, WHEN, HOW MANY
                               precise, cheap, exhaustive, every frame

    VLM LAYER (new)        ->  WHAT HAPPENED, and WAS IT NORMAL
                               semantic, expensive, ~1% of footage

### Why not "just give the video to an LLM"

Measured, not assumed:

  * "Your Vision-Language Model Can't Even Count to 20" (arXiv:2510.04401)
  * "Counting to Four is still a Chore for VLMs" (arXiv:2604.10039)
  * Gemini 3 Flash: 42.1% exact accuracy on video repetition counting.
    "VLMs can describe what happens but fail to reliably determine WHEN and
    HOW OFTEN."

A VLM would describe this lobby beautifully and count it badly. Counting is
exactly the part it is worst at, and exactly the part the business pays for.

### The annotated video is not decoration — it is the bridge

NVIDIA VSS: "CV metadata generated by object detectors and trackers is used to
IMPROVE THE ACCURACY of VLM by generating inputs with overlaid object ID and
masks — this enables Set-of-Marks prompting."

Boxes + persistent IDs + zone labels are precisely the input that makes a VLM
reliable. Shown "P3 staff" and "P7", it reasons about those PEOPLE rather than
guessing from pixels. The render we treated as optional output is the input to
tier 5.

Production still runs `--no-render` for the numbers; tier 5 renders only the
10-30s clips it needs.

## 4. Phases

    A  MEASURE          per-stage profiler: decode|detect|assoc|reid|face|render
                        ~30 lines, one 10-min run.
                        GATE: Phase C only proceeds if ReID > 30% of runtime.
                        Rationale: the bottleneck has been misdiagnosed twice
                        (detector, then tiling; it was the proxy JPEG writer).

    B  FREE SPEED       TensorRT detector (tools/export_tensorrt.py, written)
                        TensorRT ReID (what NvDeepSORT does)
                        DET_BATCH 24->48 (GPU at 6.3 of 22.5 GB)
                        DONE: proxy_render off, tile_px 1280, tile batching
                        Accuracy: unchanged by construction.

    C  SELECTIVE ReID   Embed ONLY when motion association is ambiguous:
                        crossing tracks, occlusion, re-appearance, low conf.
                        arXiv:2409.06617 (Fast-StrongSORT) · LITE arXiv:2409.04187
                        MAY IMPROVE ACCURACY HERE: every skipped embedding is a
                        noisy IR crop feeding a threshold that currently accepts
                        75% of strangers.

    D  ACCURACY         ground plane: 5 adjacent tile corners + tile size in cm
                        REID_SIM_THRESHOLD 0.37 -> 0.61 (our own EER sweep)
                        fisheye k: one full-res frame -> autocalib writes it
                        reception zone: redraw where staff actually stand
                        gt_kit + eval_harness on the eval_frames -> HOTA
                        THIS IS WHERE 90% COMES FROM. None of it is new AI.

    E  COMPRESSED GATE  CoVA (USENIX ATC'22): H.264 motion vectors WITHOUT full
                        decode. Empty hours cost almost nothing. The 24h unlock.

    F  VLM TIER 5       Anomaly + event timeline on annotated clips only.
                        clock.py already supplies real wall-clock timestamps.

## 5. Non-negotiables

* Every phase works on any video length and any fixed camera. Nothing hardcoded
  to 24h or to CAM.112.
* No change ships without the run ledger showing what it moved.
* Speed changes must be output-identical, or be declared as trades.
* "Not measured" never renders as a number.
