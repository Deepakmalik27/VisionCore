# Another major recommendation: use foot-point, not box center

For people counting and zone crossing, don't use:

```text

```

Use approximately:

```text
bottom-center of bounding box
```

because that represents where the person's feet are.

Especially with your camera angle.

Otherwise someone can visually enter a zone while the center of their bounding box hasn't crossed the boundary.

This can create incorrect IN/OUT events.
