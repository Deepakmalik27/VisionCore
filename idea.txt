The single most important structural fact: supervision is a stateless toolbox. Almost every class holds at most a small ring buffer (DetectionsSmoother: a deque per track; LineZone: a deque of booleans per track). keva_vision is a stateful evidence system — _IdentityMemory, OccupancyRecorder, LiveSeparability, DetectionFunnel, GroundPlane, the phantom suppressor all carry state for the entire run and then a whole second offline pass re-decides identity globally.

You are not competing with supervision. You are consuming it — supervision==0.26.1 is pinned in your Dockerfile, and you use exactly 10 symbols from it.

  ▸ mask memory at 4K → answered by CompactMask RLE + rle/paint

keva_vision's bottlenecks (measured, in YOUR files)
  ▸ ⛔ NO TRUSTWORTHY GROUND TRUTH. gt.txt is 600 rows of synthetic MOT — your own
    audit called it static/fake. eval/gt_entries_*.json is 3 windows. SUCCESS_CRITERIA
    says every target is "Untested (no ground truth)". Every number below this line is

  ▸ ⛔ Re-ID separability = 0.658 balanced accuracy. topology.py states it plainly:
    same p50=0.435, diff p50=0.370. "No threshold rescues that." Every merge decision
    downstream inherits a coin-flip-plus-a-bit.
  ▸ ⛔ RESOLUTION STARVATION. Decode 3840×2160, analyse at 1280–1920. Guest at door
    ≈60px, upscaled to 128×256 before the ReID backbone → the embedding is mostly
