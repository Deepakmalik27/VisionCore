37. The most important change to your mindset

Your current project should not be:

"How do I make the AI detect people better?"

It should be:

"How do I make every decision traceable, measurable, recoverable, and defensible when the vision model is wrong?"

That means:

Vision
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Tracking
  ↓
Identity
  ↓
Geometry
  ↓
Temporal reasoning
  ↓
Event
  ↓
Confidence
  ↓
Decision
  ↓
Evidence

And every step can say:

"Here's what I saw, here's what I believed, here's why I believed it, and here's how confident I am."

That is the architecture I'd build around what you already have.

I would not touch Pose, DeepStream, or new fancy models first.

My first work session would be:

1. Run the 13 sealed windows.
2. Produce detector survival audit.

Only when that baseline is stable would I move toward motion gating, IR adaptation, asynchronous processing, TensorRT/DeepStream, and finally pose/action intelligence.

That sequence gives you something much more valuable than a huge collection of ML components: a system where you can prove whether each change actually made it better.
